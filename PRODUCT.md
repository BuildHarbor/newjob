# NewJob

## Register

product

## Users

Job seekers planning their next career move at their own computer. The user requested a new brand and a free implementation of the publicly observable NxtJob product workflows.

## Product Purpose

One private workspace to plan a search, build a resume, check job fit, organize applications and contacts, and practice interviews with local AI.

## Brand Personality

Calm, capable, encouraging. The confirmed brand name is NewJob.

## Anti-references

Avoid fabricated success statistics, expensive upsells, decorative agent avatars, and a wall of identical feature cards. This is an independent implementation, not an official NxtJob client.

## Design Principles

- Help the user take the next useful action.
- Make saved work and generation status visible.
- Separate observed facts from AI suggestions.
- Keep the core experience useful without AI or internet.
- Keep user data local and exportable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable contrast, semantic controls, keyboard navigation, visible focus, mobile layouts, reduced motion, and descriptive labels.
