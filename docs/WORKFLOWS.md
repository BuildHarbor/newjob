# Company to offer: using the new workflows

1. **Settings:** hosted users load the free browser AI model. Desktop users start Ollama and choose a local model.
2. **Career evidence:** save real Challenge–Action–Result stories. These provide reusable proof for pitches and posts.
3. **Target Companies:** Discover companies → Load companies, filter loaded entries and Add to targets. Or add any company with its website.
4. **Research & pitch:** enter public URLs and excerpts. Gather sources only works without AI. Gather sources & identify problems adds AI hypotheses. Read original sources and validate assumptions.
5. **Build a Win Deck:** choose a hypothesis, review the company/job, add your approach and highlights, and optionally attach four PNG/JPG images under 300 KB each. Captions inform the text model. Generate or start with an editable outline.
6. **Deck editor:** expand each slide to edit text, notes and image placement. Refresh preview, then export editable PowerPoint or standalone HTML. Native Print/PDF depends on your browser.
7. **Offer negotiation:** complete five lessons, enter annual amounts in one currency, compare offers and practice four rounds. AI gives coaching and a simulated manager response. The wording checklist works without AI. Download the completed report.
8. **Content planner:** choose a story and topic, generate a draft, edit it, choose a date and copy when ready. Publishing is manual; dates do not send notifications.
9. **Career roadmap:** describe your priorities to generate role directions and a 14-day plan.

Browser data stays in that browser. Export a backup regularly. Neither edition sends messages or applications for you. Review all generated content and keep hypothetical company problems clearly labeled.
